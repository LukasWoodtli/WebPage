---
title: Note to Recruiters and Headhunters
---
If you are a recruiter (headhunter) and want to hire me, please read this page before you [contact me](/contact).

I am currently *not* looking for a new job. But I am always open to new and interesting challenges.

A new position must meet some requirements to catch my attention:

# Musts:

- Embedded software: I prefer firmware development over desktop or server applications. The job does not have to be only embedded software development, but it must be part of it.
- C/C++ and Rust: I like C and C++, but Rust is even better. The job must include at least some C++ or Rust development.
- Workplace in or near Zürich (max. 30 minutes by bike): I do not want to lose too much time commuting.
- Part-time work (80%): This is an absolute must. I have a family that I need and want to take care of.
- Occasional work from home (home office): Sometimes it is simply more convenient to work from home.

# Mustn'ts:

- I will never work for the weapons industry ('defence' is a euphemism): I do not like wars!


# Nice to Have

- Opportunities for sports (running in the neighbourhood, showers)
- A team lead position
